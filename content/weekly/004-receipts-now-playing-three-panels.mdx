Two things this week: a receipt flow for a client project, and a restructure of this site's home page.

## Salonjaa client portal

The portal got a printable payment receipt, then a downloadable one wired to real payment data. Getting the download right took most of the work:

- Browser print was replaced with a generated PDF.
- `next/image` was swapped for a plain `img` in the receipt, so the capture step could render the logo.
- `html2canvas` gave way to `html2canvas-pro`, which handles `oklch` and `lab` colours.
- The receipt now sits on a real A4 page with margins.

Around it: a password visibility toggle, a payment thank-you email and an invoice card.

## This site

- Site chrome moved into the root layout, with Blogs and Weekly links in the nav.
- A now-playing chip in the hero and a music-icon button in the nav with a pixel popover.
- The home page became a three-panel layout, each panel scrolling on its own with Lenis.
- Project cards turned into screenshot cards with a hover overlay. That one is still work in progress.
- Small fixes: a dead resume link, a missing hero work button, Firefox cursor visibility.
