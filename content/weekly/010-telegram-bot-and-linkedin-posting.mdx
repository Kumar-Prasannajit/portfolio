Most of the week went into the post automation. Two threads, plus some SEO.

## Telegram review bot

I wrote a spec and a plan first, then built it in small steps:

- It parses draft pull requests and their posts.
- It handles Telegram updates at `/api/telegram`.
- GitHub Actions send the draft, reminder and log messages.
- Edit comments are applied, and empty runs are reported as issues.
- Drafts now branch from `origin/main`, and the notify job checks out `main`.

Posts can also carry an optional `coverImage` now.

## LinkedIn posting

Same approach: a spec, a plan, then the parts.

- Caption text and escaping for LinkedIn's commentary field.
- A Redis store for the token, the posted records and the OAuth state.
- A REST and OAuth adapter, with connect, callback, post and remind routes.
- A Retry LinkedIn button in Telegram.
- A daily token check, and posting from the live step after a merge.

On Friday I made it claim a post before sending it and put a timeout on every outbound call. I wrote about that separately in [Claim it before you post it](/blog/claim-before-you-post).

## Salonjaa

Three SEO commits: the homepage title leads with the full brand name, a Bing Webmaster Tools verification file is added, and the site links to Facebook, Instagram and the Google Business Profile.
