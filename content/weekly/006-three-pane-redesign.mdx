I started this week with a spec (`docs: add three-pane redesign spec`) and then worked through it phase by phase.

## The phases

1. A three-axis token architecture for theming.
2. A three-pane layout shell.
3. Rail 3: live stats, ambient ASCII and a theme accordion.
4. Cursor: `mix-blend-mode: difference`.
5. A data-bound, two-paragraph About with a masked line rise.
6. A computed, self-ranking Stack section with a Work filter.
7. and 8. An editorial work feed and a live telemetry nav.
9. A playable Snake on the contribution grid.

After the phases: character images in the theme accordion panels, and the portrait tinted per character theme.

## The cursor, again

The cursor is the part I reworked most. It ended up as a square difference-blend cursor. Grid snapping and the idle blink were dropped, and the blend moved to the outer wrapper so nav items invert instead of vanishing.
