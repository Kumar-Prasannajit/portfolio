A busy week on the portfolio, with some admin work on a client project.

## This site

- **Work.** An in-flow Work section, `/work/[slug]` routes with consistent numbering, and case-study pages with real screenshots.
- **Boot screen.** A terminal-style preloader timed with CSS.
- **Motion.** Scroll reveals, hero parallax and the marquee, all on one motion system.
- **Home.** A fixed identity panel with a portrait, live time, date and viewers, a GitHub heatmap under the marquee recoloured to the site's red, and a note of where the viewer is watching from.
- **Layout.** Side panels now stay mounted across every route.
- **Quality.** A Lighthouse pass covering contrast, focus and touch targets, plus fixes for a theme hydration mismatch and a few client-side navigation links.
- **SEO.** Per-page canonicals, Open Graph images for home, blog and weekly pages, RSS feeds, sitemap dates and Vercel analytics.

I added interaction sounds early in the week, off by default, and removed the toggle and the sounds again the next day. {/* CHECK: the reason for removing the sounds isn't in the commits. */}

## Salonjaa client portal

- Admin client form: editable email, server-generated password, +91 phone.
- Password reset when a client's email changes, and a GitHub-style client delete.
- Viewable payment requests with soft-cancel, admin modals portaled to `document.body`, and an admin receipt download.
