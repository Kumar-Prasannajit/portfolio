Mostly phone fixes this week, plus new content and two new repos.

## Portfolio

- **Mobile.** The phone strip shows the time and date unclipped. "VIEWERS" stays inside its tile in brutal mode at 320px. The Stack section shows two tools per row, and the phone menu now lists Specs and Places.
- **Theme and rails.** The light and dark wave no longer lags. Rail 3 shows only real data, and a show that is still airing no longer reads "/ ?".
- **Stack.** The tiles show only "last used".
- **Hero.** I dropped the `[ HYDERABAD, IN ]` chip.
- **Gallery.** Real photos as WebP, shown uncropped.
- **Work and Stack.** A RAG chatbot project, and PostgreSQL, Redis, BullMQ and Docker are pinned.
- **Places.** Kerala, North East and Dubai are in, New York is out.
